Connections Privacy

Last updated October 5, 2026

Connections is a shared wall for a community of friends. It has no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own.

What you share, and who sees it

Your name, profile photo, bio, posts, photos, comments and reactions are shared with everyone who uses Connections. Please only post what you are happy for the whole community to see.

Where it's stored

Everything is stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) in the app's public database. Connections uses your iCloud account only to know which posts are yours; it never sees your Apple ID, email address or password. Photos are re-saved without location or camera details before they are uploaded.

Reports and blocking

When you report a post, comment or person, the report (with a copy of the reported text) is visible only to you and the developer, who reviews reports within 24 hours and removes content and people that break the Community Rules. Blocking someone is stored on your phone and hides them from you.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, posts, photos, comments, reactions and reports. Deleting the app alone does not delete what you posted.

Testing

If you use Connections through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to send with the developer, as described in TestFlight's own terms.

Children

Connections is not meant for children under 13.
