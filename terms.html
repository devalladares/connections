Community Rules

Last updated October 5, 2026

Connections is a wall for friends. By joining, you agree to these rules. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive people.

Be kind

  • No harassment, bullying, threats or hate based on who someone is.
  • No nudity, sexual content, or graphic violence.
  • No spam, scams, or impersonating someone else.
  • Only post photos you have the right to share, and don't share other people's private information.
  • Nothing illegal.

Report and block

Every post, comment and profile has Report and Block. Reported content is hidden from you right away. The developer reviews every report within 24 hours, removes content that breaks these rules, and removes people who post it.

Your content

You own what you post. You let Connections show it to the community. You can delete any post or comment, or your whole account, at any time from inside the app.

No warranty

Connections is a small app made for friends and is provided as is. It may change or stop working.
